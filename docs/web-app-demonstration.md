# ScanTrack Web App: Features and Demo Guide

## Introduction

ScanTrack is a terminal operations system for TODA organizations. It brings driver and vehicle records, QR-based entry and exit logging, attendance, and operational reports into one place. Administrators oversee the system, while managers handle day-to-day work for their organization.

## Main features

### Dashboard

The dashboard gives staff a quick view of terminal operations. It summarizes key counts, lists registered organizations, and shows recent QR scans and system activity. The summary figures refresh automatically, so the dashboard can help staff monitor changes without manually reloading the page.

### Driver management

Administrators and managers can review and maintain driver records, including driver details and assigned vehicle or organization information. The administrator dashboard also provides driver search, making it easier to locate a record.

### QR credential management

Drivers use QR credentials to identify themselves during terminal scans. Authorized staff can manage a driver's QR code by activating it, deactivating it, or generating a replacement. This helps keep the scan process tied to current driver credentials.

### Vehicle management

Staff can view and maintain vehicle records and their details. Keeping vehicle information alongside driver records helps staff understand which vehicle is associated with a driver.

### QR scans and attendance

When a driver's QR code is scanned, the system records terminal activity such as an entry or exit. Staff can review recent scans on the dashboard and use the attendance area to inspect recorded attendance.

### Reports and analytics

Reports help staff review operational records beyond the latest dashboard activity. Depending on access, users can view or export reports and use analytics to understand activity over time.

### Manager portal, schedules, and queue

Managers have an organization-focused portal for handling their drivers, vehicles, QR credentials, attendance, and reports. The portal also includes schedule management and queue monitoring, supporting day-to-day terminal coordination.

### Organization settings

Administrators can view and manage TODA organization information. This keeps organization names and travel-area details available to the rest of the system.

### System health and activity history

The System Health page provides an overview of the application's operational status. Recent system activity gives staff a record of events such as account, verification, and terminal activity.

## Suggested demonstration

1. Start on the **dashboard** and explain the summary, recent scans, and activity history.
2. Open **drivers** and **vehicles** to show how records are organized and connected.
3. Visit **QR management** and explain how credentials are activated, deactivated, or replaced.
4. Show **attendance** and **reports** to demonstrate how staff review terminal activity.
5. If manager access is available, show the manager portal's organization-specific tools, schedules, and queue.
6. Finish with **organization settings** and **System Health**.

## Closing

ScanTrack connects driver and vehicle records with QR-based terminal activity. Its dashboard, attendance, and reports help administrators and managers keep operations organized and make recorded activity easier to review.
